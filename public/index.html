    🚙🚙🚙 Convoy trips

    We don't just travel. We roll out together.

    Long trips run as a convoy: guided vehicles moving as one, with a planned route, planned stops and a team watching the whole line.

    • Lead & sweep vehicles

      A guide's vehicle sets the pace up front and a sweep vehicle closes the line, so nobody gets left behind.

    • Drivers on radio

      Every driver stays connected the whole way, for road updates, wildlife sightings and quick re-routes.

    • Planned stops

      Viewpoints, fuel, nyama choma and stretch breaks at places we trust, timed into the route.

    • Safety first

      Experienced drivers, well-kept vehicles and a first-aid kit in every one. You just enjoy the ride.
